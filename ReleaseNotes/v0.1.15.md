# Portfolix v0.1.15

## 更新内容

- 升级「AI 投研」体验与 Agent 架构：支持多会话线程管理、本地投研记忆、投顾角色定制、交错式工具调用与时间线进度展示、自适应输入框，以及 Antigravity MCP 集成；修复无 Key 状态提示与模型输出解析边界问题。
- 重构「风险画像」单页风控护栏工作台：升级为 6 项核心风控护栏与三档风险状态，支持草稿编辑、未保存导航拦截与备份往返还原。
- 新增只读「演示模式」（设置 → 数据管理）：一键载入 30 天多币种模拟组合与预置投资分布，支持安全体验完整功能而不影响个人账本。
- 统一视觉与双语语义体系：采用黑曜紫晶（Obsidian Amethyst）深浅色配色方案，统一弹窗与主窗口玻璃材质、AI 头像与侧边栏图标品牌渐变、全应用文案规范，并将投资云图「今日盈亏」面积改为按当日盈亏绝对值加权。
- 优化首日账本体验与数据一致性：新账本首日完整展示盈亏日历网格与投资分布回退，修正同日余额校准时的今日盈亏计算一致性、人民币基准账本币种换算，以及后台自动更新注册失败时的状态回滚与中文错误提示。
- 完善持仓操作、交易表单与双语本地化细节：包括未选中持仓时的按钮禁用态、清零持仓二次确认、持仓搜索空状态、交易单价精度与候选列表交互优化、现金账户动态双语命名与精确时间戳展示。

## What's New

- Upgraded the AI Research Agent with multi-thread conversations, local research memory, advisor persona customization, interleaved tool calls with timeline progress, an adaptive composer, and Antigravity MCP integration, alongside missing-key and response-parsing fixes.
- Redesigned Risk Profile into a single-page guardrail workspace featuring 6 core guardrails, 3-tier severity states, draft/save workflows with unsaved-change protection, and full backup round-trip support.
- Added a read-only 30-day Demo Mode (Settings → Data Management) with a realistic multi-currency portfolio and built-in investment distribution so you can explore the full app without affecting your personal ledger.
- Unified the visual and bilingual copy system around the Obsidian Amethyst light/dark palette, consistent glass materials across sheets and windows, matching brand gradients for AI avatars and the sidebar icon, refined terminology, and daily P&L Investment Cloud weighting by absolute P&L amount.
- Improved first-day dashboard usability and ledger consistency, including full P&L calendar grids on day one, Investment Map auto-fallback, consistent daily P&L handling alongside same-day reconciliations, CNY base-currency accounting fixes, and reliable background price updater rollback with localized errors.
- Refined holdings actions, trade forms, and localization details: disabled states when no position is selected, zero-quantity clear confirmation, position search empty states, improved trade unit-price precision and candidate-list scrolling, dynamic bilingual cash account names, and unified trade timestamps.
