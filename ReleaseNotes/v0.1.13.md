# Portfolix v0.1.13

## 更新内容

- 新账本默认提供 Apple、NVIDIA、BTC 三项示例持仓，可自行编辑或删除；已有账本保持原样。
- 每次完全退出后重新打开 App（包括首次打开），自动刷新一次价格，独立于后台定时刷新策略。
- 新增 Exa、AnySearch 搜索支持，支持多个搜索来源并发调用，改善追问时的搜索主题、引用与次数统计。
- 优化 AI 回答格式解析和引用展示，减少异常内容对阅读的干扰。
- 选择器和侧边栏加入流畅的紫色滑动选中效果，移除侧边栏的灰色悬停背景。
- 无盈亏记录时仍显示完整日历、柱状图坐标和收益趋势零线，并优化首页日期文字与金额显示。

## What's New

- New ledgers start with editable Apple, NVIDIA, and BTC sample holdings. Existing ledgers remain unchanged.
- Supported asset prices refresh once on every fresh app launch, including the first launch, independently of background updates.
- Added Exa and AnySearch, concurrent search providers, and improved follow-up search relevance, citations, and search statistics.
- Improved AI response parsing and citation display for more readable answers.
- Added smooth purple selection animations to selectors and the sidebar, and removed the sidebar's gray hover background.
- Kept the calendar, bar-chart axes, and return-trend zero line visible without P&L history, and refined dashboard date and amount typography.
